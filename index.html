UtilizeReach

Self-hosted, source-available cold-email & lead automation — own your data.

An AI-assisted platform for lead capture and permission-based cold outreach that runs entirely on your own server. No per-seat pricing, no vendor lock-in, no data leaving your infrastructure.

self-hosted · source-available · your data on your server

Everything you need to run outreach — on your own stack

FastAPI + PostgreSQL + Redis/Celery on the backend, React 19 on the front. Sending via the Gmail API. Bring your own LLM — Claude, Gemini, OpenAI, or any OpenAI-compatible endpoint.

AI email generation

Personalized copy written per lead using your chosen LLM, with campaign A/B variants baked in.

Multi-inbox personas

Send as multiple Gmail send-as aliases from one account — distinct sender personas, one deployment.

Warm-up sender

Paced daily volume with optional auto-ramp to protect deliverability while you scale.

Campaigns, A/B & follow-ups

Sequence multi-step campaigns with A/B variants and automatic follow-ups until a reply lands.

Tracking & bounce handling

Open, click and thread-based reply detection with automatic bounce quarantine — undelivered mail never counts as engagement.

Lead scraper & segments

Search-driven scraping via SerpAPI plus CSV/Excel import, organized into modular, reusable segments.

Period-aware analytics

Deliverability and engagement funnels sliced by persona, segment and campaign over any date range.

Unsubscribe & compliance

One-click unsubscribe, exclusion lists and UTM-attributed capture forms built in for permission-based outreach.

An open-source alternative to Instantly, Lemlist, Smartlead & Apollo

Those are capable, proprietary cloud products. UtilizeReach is the option for teams who would rather run the whole thing themselves — same core outreach workflow, on infrastructure you control.
